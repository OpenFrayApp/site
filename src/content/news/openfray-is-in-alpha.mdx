The goblin’s next attack has disadvantage. The ogre’s reaction is spent. Another
creature needs a saving throw when its turn starts. OpenFray keeps those details
beside the initiative order while you run the fight.

The first public build is a free combat console for Game Masters running DnD 5e.
It opens in a browser tab, with nothing to install and no account required.
This is an alpha release, so expect rough edges.

[Open the console](/console/) and put your party and a few creatures on the board.

## Keep the fight in view

The console is one screen, three columns: everyone in the fight down the left, the
selected creature's stat block in the middle, its controls and the log on the right.

![The console mid-fight: initiative order on the left, a Frightened and Prone Ogre's stat block in the middle, its effects and the log on the right.](../../assets/screenshots/combat.png)

Click **Begin** and OpenFray asks for everyone's initiative. It rolls the creatures and
leaves the players blank for you to type what they rolled. From there, moving to the next
turn does more than move a marker. Timed effects count themselves down and clear,
creatures get their reaction back, recharge abilities roll to see whether they return, and
a creature makes the saving throw that ends an effect at the start or the end of its turn,
whichever you chose.

Add a creature mid-fight or move a row: the turn stays with the creature taking it.
Fallen creatures stay in the order, grayed out and skipped, in case they come back.

## Remember what changes the next roll

Reckless Attack goes on the barbarian and grants advantage to whoever swings at them.
Vicious Mockery goes on the goblin and changes the goblin’s next attack. OpenFray
works both into the roll when it happens.

The same effect controls cover conditions, advantage, disadvantage, bonuses, penalties,
reminders, and effects that end with a saving throw. An effect can change a creature’s
own rolls or the rolls made **against** it.

Anything the console doesn’t know about becomes a reminder in your own words. It shows on the
row until you clear it, and it applies nothing. You decide what it means.

## What creatures have left to spend

A creature's resources run down as you use them, and come back when they should: legendary
actions and their per-round budget, legendary resistance with its separate in-lair count,
recharge abilities, limited-use actions, spell uses counted per spell, and the one reaction
each creature gets per round.

Spellcasting is live from the stat block. Click a spell and OpenFray fills in that
creature's save DC and attack bonus, spends the use, and routes the spell into the attack
box or the group save. A concentration spell starts the caster concentrating, with a timer.

## Resolve the Fireball together

Attacks resolve from the stat block: pick a target, roll to hit against its armor class,
then apply damage that already accounts for what the target resists, is immune to, or is
vulnerable to. Critical hits follow your table's rule.

**Group save** handles every target of a Fireball together. Each creature makes its
own saving throw, with its bonus, Magic Resistance, and Evasion accounted for.
Apply the damage in one step, full or half per creature. A creature with legendary
resistance can turn a failed save into a success in the same box.

The log shows the dice behind each result. Rolls use the browser’s cryptographic
random generator, with no smoothing of streaks.

Players roll their own dice. Wherever OpenFray would roll for a creature, it asks you what
the player got instead.

## Your creatures, your table

The built-in compendium carries the SRD's creatures and spells, searchable, with full stat
blocks and spell cards. Beyond it:

- **Build your own.** A full stat-block editor for creatures and a card editor for spells.
  You give the ability scores and the challenge rating; OpenFray derives the attack
  bonuses, save and skill bonuses, and the spell save DC from them.
- **Save your players.** Build a character once, with armor class, hit points, abilities,
  defenses, senses, and private notes, then drop them into any fight instead of typing
  them again.
- **Campaigns.** Your table's rulings, set once: critical hit damage, how surprise works,
  how creature hit points are rolled, and how initiative ties break. Every fight you run
  under that campaign uses them.

## What the alpha leaves for later

The alpha has no shared read-only player view. A second screen showing turn order and
conditions to the table is planned for a later release.

If a rule behaves incorrectly or a creature renders badly, report it in the
[issue tracker](https://github.com/OpenFrayApp/console/issues).

## Bring your table back next time

You can run a fight without an account. A free account keeps your creatures, spells,
characters, and campaigns available between devices, so you can reuse your party
and your table’s house rules.

[Open the console](/console/) to run your first fight. Sign in with Google or Discord
when you want to keep your table’s material for another session.
