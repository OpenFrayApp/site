The crop woke up. [The Waking Garden](/the-waking-garden/) is a bestiary of sixty-seven
creatures for a world where the things people grow have started growing back — and it is
published here in full, free to read, whether or not you ever open the console.

It is written for the 2024 rules and works with 2014. The stat blocks are tuned to 5.5e,
so a few will hit harder than their challenge rating suggests at a 5e table.

## Three stages, and what each is for

The plants are sorted by how far they have got, which turns out to be the same thing as
sorting them by tier of play.

| Stage                 | Levels | What it is                                                |
| --------------------- | ------ | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Stage 1: rooted**   | 1–3    | Awake, aware, and unable to leave the furrow they grew in |
| **Stage 2: uprooted** | 4–8    | Torn free of the soil, and hunting                        |
| **Stage 3: crowned**  | 9+     | Sovereign, legendary, and the thing a campaign ends on    |

The first stage is the interesting one to run, because none of it can chase you. A
Speartip Row has a 15-foot reach and a Speed of 0. A Grinning Gourd sits in the furrow
looking like the rest of the crop until the light comes on behind its eyes. The fight
exists because of where it happens, not because something crossed the field to start it —
so the terrain is doing the work that a monster's movement usually does.

By stage 3 that has inverted. Thirteen creatures in the library have legendary actions,
and the last of them is a CR 22 plant that every waking garden turns out to be a cutting
from.

## The rest of the field

Two chapters sit outside the three stages, and both exist to stop the garden reading as
one note.

**The rest of the garden** is eleven weeds, pests and scavengers that live alongside the
crop — several of which would rather eat a vegetable than a person, which makes them
something other than a second wave of enemies.

**The Gardener and its retinue** is who has been doing this on purpose: a fey with an
under-gardener, a rookery of crows, and six garden tools it wakes when it wants hands.

## Nineteen encounters, already built

The last appendix is [encounter seeds](/the-waking-garden/appendix-a/): nineteen
encounters from level 1 to level 20, each with the idea it is built around, its terrain,
and its roster with placements. They are there because in this library the terrain usually
_is_ the encounter, and a roster on its own does not carry that.

There are also two indexes, by challenge rating and by creature type, so you can find
something at the right size without reading a chapter to get to it.

## Using it at the table

Open **Settings** in [the console](/console/) — the gear at the top right — and tick **The
Waking Garden**. Its creatures then appear in **Add creature** alongside everything else,
with full stat blocks, rollable actions, and their own source line.

Every stat block on this site renders from the same file the console loads, so the page
you read and the creature you add can't disagree with each other.
