[Brood & Bloom](/brood-and-bloom/) shipped with three broods of parasites and the order of
physicians that catalogs them. It now has the people who disagree with that order, the
medicine both sides carry, and the magic nobody publishes.

The library goes from sixty-one creatures to sixty-seven, and gains nineteen alchemical
preparations and twenty-three spells. It is still free to read in full, and the spells are
now castable in [the console](/console/).

## A cult, in six stat blocks

Chapter 3 is **The Hands of the Host**. Its founder was an officer of the Lazaret who spent
eleven years copying case records, drew the opposite conclusion from them, published it as
doctrine requires, and then walked out with a hundred and forty pages. What he published
was an argument that a graft is not an affliction but an offer.

The six stat blocks range from CR 1 to CR 15:

| Creature            | CR  | What they do                                                   |
| ------------------- | --- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Almoner**         | 1   | Uninfected. Arrives during an outbreak with real medicine      |
| **Postulant**       | 2   | The newest grade. Digs, carries, holds doors                   |
| **Sexton**          | 6   | Keeps the ground, and gets bodies to it before the day is out  |
| **Ostiary**         | 8   | The doorkeeper. Performs the rite and keeps the clutch         |
| **Ciborium**        | 12  | The third grade, seated, counting the depth of its own case    |
| **The First Graft** | 15  | The author. A tired administrator who asks about your symptoms |

The Hands prefer conversation to opening a fight. An almoner can first appear as a
source of medicine during an outbreak. Hands surrender readily, talk freely, and
treat capture as a chance to explain their doctrine.

The Ciborium tracks Depth, spends it to seed the party, and takes it back from
anybody standing nearby, including its own hands, who thank it for the attention.

## Nineteen jars

Chapter 7 opens with alchemy, and none of it needs a spellcaster. Every preparation is
rendered from one of the three broods, and the differences decide what a party can keep on
hand: Sporophore material stockpiles for a month, Inquiline material lasts an hour off a
living parasite, and Necrophore material needs a fresh death.

There are draughts, unguents, fumes and poisons, from an 8 sp fume that keeps driftlings
off a camp to a 750 gp injury poison. Three of them have no price at all.

The preparations use familiar item actions. Drinking a draught takes a Bonus Action,
as does coating a weapon; the coating lasts 1 minute. Throwing a preparation replaces
an attack, as with Alchemist’s Fire.

## Twenty-three spells, and who has them

The spells come from the order, the cult, and lone practitioners’ notebooks. None
are published within the setting. Each entry names its origin, which determines
where a party might find it and who might teach it.

| Level    | Spells                                                            |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cantrips | Anamnesis, Douter, Fair Copy, Latchwork, Unction                  |
| 1        | Brood Inquest, Countenance, Exacerbation, Lazaret Sill            |
| 2        | Assumption of the Case, Bloom Interdict, Discharge, Efflorescence |
| 3        | Chantry Tithe, False Wakelight, Glebe, Prosector's Purgation      |
| 4        | Instar, Second Assignment, Sequestration                          |
| 5        | Extirpation, Laying-In, Preferment                                |

Three spells require the caster to carry a graft, which the cult considers proof of
everything it teaches. Extirpation ends a case at any stage short of the last, but
leaves the fully grown parasite in the room with you.

Brood & Bloom is the first library beyond Basic Rules 2024 and Basic Rules 2014 to
include castable spells. Four offer to apply their effects to the board; the rest
require the Game Master to resolve their effects.

## What moved

The book was rearranged to make room, so a bookmark may have shifted:

- **The Lazaret** has its own chapter now, at 2, instead of sharing chapter 1 with
  everything else.
- **Growth and reproduction** moved out of the appendix and into chapter 1, next to the
  rules it explains.
- **The three broods** are chapters 4, 5 and 6.
- **Appendix C** is a new index: every preparation with what it does, and every spell twice
  over, by level and by the class that can learn it.

Three creatures from the earlier chapters picked up spells. The **Orcshroom Sporespeaker**
casts Glebe, which makes contaminated ground where there was none. The **Psalter Tick** can
spend a stolen spell slot on Chantry Tithe, which rewards it for staying near your
casters. The **Choirgall** gains Bloom Interdict.

The **Lazaret officers** carry the order's own counter-work, split by rank. The registrar
surveys, the prosector works in the field, the lector holds what a house does behind its
own doors, and they have gear lines now.

## Reading it

The whole library is on the site, and the print edition is 101 pages. Turn it on in the
console under **Settings**, and its creatures join **Add creature** with everything else.
