[Brood & Bloom](/brood-and-bloom/) is a bestiary of sixty-one creatures whose lives run
through other bodies, living, dead or built. Like [The Waking
Garden](/the-waking-garden/), it is published here in full and free to read, and it is
built into the console.

An infection can remain with the party after the fight ends.

## Three broods that want different things

The three broods are unrelated. They infect different hosts and places:

| Brood          | Chapter | What it is                                                             |
| -------------- | ------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Inquiline**  | 2       | Infects one person. What a party carries away                          |
| **Sporophore** | 3       | Infects ground and buildings. What a party walks into                  |
| **Necrophore** | 4       | Wants nothing from the living. What a party leaves behind, in its dead |

The first two broods use counters the console can hold for you. The Inquiline tracks
**Depth**, which never recedes on its own: the graft has to come out. The Sporophore
tracks **Spore Load**, which clears with rest and distance from the colony.
The Necrophore uses neither counter; it inhabits the dead the party leaves behind.

## Read chapter 1 first

Several creatures affect their hosts between encounters. Their stat blocks rely on
chapter 1 for classifications, counters, disease stages, and the spells that help.
One spell can worsen a particular case; chapter 1 explains that interaction too.

That framing chapter is also where the order lives. The Lazaret has been writing this down
for longer than anyone remembers. Its three ranks have stat blocks: a **Registrar**
at CR 1, a **Prosector** at CR 4, and a **Lector** at CR 5.

## From nothing to an apex

The Inquiline opens on a CR 0 latchling and ends on the
**Exemplar** at CR 17; the Sporophore runs from a driftling to the **Nave**; the Necrophore
from a cinder nit to the **Lychfield**. An appendix lays all three life cycles out
together, showing what grows into what, where it settles, and which hosts it uses.

## Using it at the table

Open **Settings** in [the console](/console/) and tick **Brood & Bloom**. Its creatures
join **Add creature** with everything else.

The [counters](/docs/guides/effects/#add-a-counter) are worth setting up as you go. Select the
character, click **Apply effect**, set **Duration** to **Counter**, and type `Depth` or
`Spore Load` into **Reminder**. It starts at 0 and sits on their row as a badge with the
number in it, with **+1**, **−1** and **Reset** beside it in the controls.

You change the counters manually. Use the library’s disease rules to determine
what happens at each value.
