Awake, aware, and unable to leave. A Stage 1 plant will sit in its furrow for fifty years if the soil never gives it more than it needs.

Nearly every Stage 1 creature has the **Rooted** trait, printed in full on each stat block that carries it: its Speed is 0 and can’t be increased, and it can’t be moved against its will or have the Prone condition imposed on it while it has more than 0 Hit Points.

The exceptions are the [**Rollrind**](#c-rollrind) and the [**Podswarm**](#c-podswarm), which move freely, and the [**Runt Patch**](#c-runt-patch), which drags its roots along behind it.

### Stage 1 and CR

A creature with Speed 0 is a hazard, not an encounter: anyone can walk around it. Every creature in this chapter earns its listed challenge rating only when one of these is true:

- **Terrain forces contact.** A walled kitchen garden, a greenhouse, a hedge maze, furrows narrow enough that a 10-foot reach covers the path.
- **The plants are the objective.** The party needs them harvested, cleared, or taken alive.
- **They’re guarding something.** A Stage 1 patch around a cache is a cheap, patient alarm that never sleeps and can’t be bribed.
- **They’re supporting something mobile.** One [Stalkstrider](/the-waking-garden/chapter-3/#c-stalkstrider) among four rooted Maize Sentinels is a better fight than either alone.

In open ground with no clock, halve their effective challenge rating. The ash-grown plants are the exception, because they move.
