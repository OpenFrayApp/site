A plant that has taken more from the soil than the soil was meant to give tears itself free, trailing a taproot it will never fully shed. It walks badly and it does not sleep.

Stage 2 creatures are ambush predators in cultivated land, and territorial toward each other — which is why parties rarely meet more than two or three of the same species together.

Any Stage 2 creature can be found **bedded down**: rooted for a season in order to advance, using the **Rooted** trait from Chapter 2 while keeping all its Stage 2 statistics. That is the most vulnerable it will ever be, and the best opportunity a party will ever get to kill something before it becomes a problem.
