## Animated Tools

The Gardener casts Animate Objects on whatever is leaning against the shed wall. Six of those results are statted below, so nobody has to open the generic animated-object rules mid-fight.

All six are built on the same chassis — the same _Antimagic Susceptibility_, the same _False Appearance_, both printed in full on each block — and differ entirely in what they do with it.

The composition of a tool encounter should be a piece of scenery the party walked past ten minutes earlier. Let them notice the rack. Let them do nothing about it.

| Tool                  | CR  | Does                                  |
| --------------------- | :-: | ------------------------------------- |
| Animated Shears       |  1  | Strips gear off people                |
| Animated Spade        |  1  | Digs pits, knocks people into them    |
| Animated Hoe          |  1  | Wrecks the ground, punishes the Prone |
| Animated Watering Can |  1  | Support. Heals other plants           |
| Animated Scythe       |  2  | Reach, sweeps two targets at once     |
| Animated Hose         |  3  | Long-range grapple, pins, drowns      |
