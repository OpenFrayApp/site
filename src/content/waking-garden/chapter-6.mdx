Chapter 1 lists _deliberate cultivation_ as the most interesting reason a plant advances. This chapter is who has been doing it.

Everything here either works for the [Gardener](#c-gardener) or has been drawn in by it, and unlike most of Chapter 5, all of it will come at the party. One entry is deliberately non-lethal: the [**Tithe-Crow**](#c-tithe-crow) doesn’t want to kill anyone, it wants something they’re carrying, and it will trade.

The Gardener’s _animate objects_ results are statted as six separate implements rather than one entry with a table, because a hose and a pair of shears are not the same creature.

**Run the [Under-Gardener](#c-under-gardener) before the Gardener.** They share the _Patient_ trait, and the lieutenant’s version is stripped down enough that a party can learn the mechanic from a fight that costs them hit points instead of a character.

<Group title="Animated tools">

The Gardener casts _animate objects_ on whatever is leaning against the shed wall. Six of those results are statted below, so nobody has to open the generic animated-object rules mid-fight.

All six are built on the same chassis — the same _Antimagic Susceptibility_, the same _False Appearance_, both printed in full on each block — and differ entirely in what they do with it.

The composition of a tool encounter should be a piece of scenery the party walked past ten minutes earlier. Let them notice the rack. Let them do nothing about it.

| Tool                  | CR  | Does                                  |
| --------------------- | :-: | ------------------------------------- |
| Animated Shears       |  1  | Strips gear off people                |
| Animated Spade        |  1  | Digs pits, knocks people into them    |
| Animated Hoe          |  1  | Wrecks the ground, punishes the Prone |
| Animated Watering Can |  1  | Support. Heals other plants           |
| Animated Scythe       |  2  | Reach, sweeps two targets at once     |
| Animated Hose         |  3  | Long-range grapple, pins, drowns      |
