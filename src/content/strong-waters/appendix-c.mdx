This book runs in the [OpenFray console](/console/) as spells and presets rather than as
stat blocks, because an apothecary's book has no creatures in it. Nothing in this appendix
is a new rule. The counts are the ones [the first chapter](/strong-waters/chapter-1/) and
[the fourth](/strong-waters/chapter-4/) set out, and what follows only says where each of
them sits on the screen.

## Turning the book on

The library is off until a Game Master asks for it:

1. Open the gear menu at the top right of the console, and choose **Settings**.
2. On the **Libraries** tab, tick **On Strong Waters and Potent Simples**.
3. Close with **Done**.

Its eleven spells join the **Cast spell** list and its presets join the **Presets** picker
inside **Apply effect**. It ships no creatures, so the **Add creature** list is unchanged.

## Which preset is which

Eight presets carry the three counts. They are found by opening **Apply effect** on any
creature or player character and searching the **Presets** picker at the top of the box:

| In the book                 | The preset                                     | What it is                                    |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Intoxication, levels 1 to 4 | **Intoxication 1** to **Intoxication 4**       | One at a time, each adding to the ones below  |
| Craving                     | **Craving**                                    | A counter, raised and lowered by hand         |
| The three degrees           | **Addicted 1**, **Addicted 2**, **Addicted 3** | A record, left on the creature between fights |

Each preset carries its own step of the rule as a reminder, and a condition only where the
book gives it one: **Intoxication 3** applies Poisoned and **Intoxication 4** applies
Unconscious. The ladder in full is the waters card in
[appendix B](/strong-waters/appendix-b/), which is where it is read; a badge on a tracker
row has space for one line.

The Advantage and Disadvantage in these rules is tied to particular abilities — Wisdom
checks, Dexterity saving throws — and the console's own modifiers do not divide that
finely, so those lines are written into the reminder rather than applied. The console
shows them; it does not roll them for anybody.

## An evening

Intoxication moves in one direction over a few hours and then goes. A level applied to a
combatant appears under **Applied effects**, and on the creature's row in the tracker:

1. Apply **Intoxication 1** on the first failed saving throw past capacity.
2. On the next failed saving throw, apply the next level and **Clear** the one below it. A
   creature holds one level at a time, and the effects of the levels below it hold too.
3. **Clear** the last of it when the evening has worn off. A creature that reached level 4
   is owed 1 Exhaustion level, which the console does not add on its own.

Applying a level that carries a condition leaves two entries rather than one — the
condition and the reminder — and both are cleared separately.

## Days, and what is kept between them

Craving and the degrees outlast a fight, so they are kept on a player character's row
rather than on a creature that will be gone by the next session.

Apply **Craving** once, and it sits under **Applied effects** with **−1**, **+1** and
**Reset** beside it, reading its count on the creature's row. Raise it by what an entry
names, lower it by 1 for a day gone entirely without, and at 6 press **Reset** and apply
the degree the count has reached. Applying **Addicted 1**, **2** or **3** records the
degree, and it stays until the addiction ends.

The console keeps a count, not a substance. A creature that wants two things needs two
counters, and two badges both reading Craving are not told apart. A Game Master tracking
more than one can stage a counter by hand and press **Save as preset** to name it for the
substance, which needs an account.

## The spells

The eleven spells of [the eighth chapter](/strong-waters/chapter-8/) appear in **Cast
spell**, badged SW&PS and linked back to the book. Casting one shows its card and records
the cast in the game log, and does nothing else: none of them rolls damage or asks for a
target, because none of them does anything to the board. What each does afterwards is
settled at the table.

## What the console does not do

Three of the book's clocks are the Game Master's, and the console holds none of them:

- **The hour.** Intoxication falls by a level for each hour without drinking. Nothing
  ticks it down; the level is replaced or cleared by hand.
- **The day.** Craving falls by 1 for each full day gone entirely without, and a degree
  ends on successes counted across days. Days pass between sessions, and a counter that
  moved on its own would be wrong more often than right.
- **The saving throw.** The one that refuses a measure and the one that ends an addiction
  are both made at the table, by whoever the day belongs to.
