The three broods reproduce in three different ways and on three different clocks. This
appendix collects all of it in one place.

## Inquiline

A latchling grows where it feeds: after 1d4 + 2 days attached, it becomes the form
its host's highest ability score sets, and every form after it grows the same way —
attached and feeding — until a sated adult leaves its host to lay:

| The host's highest ability score     | First form    | Later stages                         |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Strength, Dexterity, or Constitution | Mudspit Cyst  | Sallow Fluke, Mire Ambulant, Quagdam |
| Intelligence                         | Chantry Louse | Psalter Tick, Choirgall              |
| Wisdom or Charisma                   | Gaol Worm     | Palimpsest Wyrm                      |

Every sated adult lays a clutch of latchlings on its line's ground — still fresh water
for the sallow line, spell-soaked places for the chantry, wherever the dead are kept
for the palimpsest — and the cycle begins again.

Grafts are the second route. A graft is a cutting of its parent, and one that reaches
stage 4 of its disease emerges as a full member of its line, skipping the small forms:
a sallow graft matures in the corpse into a Quagdam, a chantry graft withdraws as a
Psalter Tick, and a palimpsest graft uncoils as a Palimpsest Wyrm. A graft whose host
dies earlier starves with it — a chantry or palimpsest graft dies within 1d4 days of
its host, while a sallow graft keeps eating and matures in the corpse into a Quagdam.

## Sporophore

A driftling that settles undisturbed for 24 hours becomes the stage 2 creature for
whatever it landed on:

| Substrate        | Line      | Stage 2        |
| ---------------- | --------- | -------------- |
| Wet tissue       | Rotgill   | Rotgill Fleece |
| Burned ground    | Ashcap    | Ashcap Crust   |
| Stone and timber | Reredos   | Reredos Rind   |
| A living person  | Orcshroom | Spore-Struck   |

From the mat, each line grows on its own ladder — the mats spread, fruiting bodies
rise out of established mats, and the apexes are not a stage any single creature
reaches but a thing an old colony becomes:

| Line      | Mat            | Fruiting body  | The colony becomes                                                                      |
| --------- | -------------- | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rotgill   | Rotgill Fleece | Rotgill Bell   | A Miasm — condensed out of a colony that filled a flooded, sealed place                 |
| Ashcap    | Ashcap Crust   | Ashcap Censer  | A Burnt Acre — a colony burned over enough times that burning became part of it         |
| Reredos   | Reredos Rind   | Reredos Corbel | A Nave — a colony that took a whole structure and stopped being distinguishable from it |
| Orcshroom | —              | —              | A Rootfather — the tribe's eldest, taken root                                           |

The orcshroom line grows through people rather than places: whelps are born to the
tribe, Metaplasia recruits everyone else, and the Spore-Struck is the swifter taking of
a living body by a settled driftling. The intermediate fighting stages of every line —
stalkers, veils, blightboars, cinderwalks, the chorus, the buttress — are colonists of
particular bodies and places, described with their stat blocks.

## Necrophore

One loop, one method, one clock:

| Adult           | Lays in          | Larvae             | Time          |
| --------------- | ---------------- | ------------------ | ------------- |
| Emberwing       | Any fresh corpse | 1d4 Cinder Nits    | 1d6 + 2 hours |
| Tallow Imago    | Any fresh corpse | 1d4 Gravewax Grubs | 1d6 + 2 hours |
| Reliquary Imago | Any fresh corpse | 1d4 Crypt Instars  | 1d6 + 2 hours |

A larva reduced to 0 Hit Points by anything but its line's weakness seals into a husk
and opens as its adult 1 hour later — metamorphosis, not reproduction; no new creature
is added by it. Two lines carry intermediate growth: a Crypt Instar that feeds long
enough grows into a Sepulchre Nymph before any pupation, and gravewax grubs in a rich
larder fuse into a Gravewax Mass or settle into a Tallow Font, either of which spills
grubs again when broken. The elders — the Pyre Matron and the Catafalque Moth — are
adults that outlived enough layings, laying on the same schedule as every other adult.
Wakelight Wisps are shed by adults and grow into nothing. A Lych Lantern is a husk that
failed, and a Lychfield is what a killing ground becomes when the whole loop runs in
one place for generations.
